## How to read a scene block

- Header: `sceneID · beat · KIND · film-time in–out`. A ⚑ after the header marks a flagged scene.
- **Narration** is verbatim and word-timed — it is the film's clock.
- **On screen** lists each shot: source title · in–out inside the mirror file · face-verification score · tier · section. Scores: `0.55+` verified Ahyeon, `0.45–0.55` likely, `0.30–0.45` group/wide, below `0.30` other/wide.
- ⚑ repeat lines mean the Director reused one source window for several consecutive cuts — prime re-brief targets.
- **SWAP OPTIONS** (flagged scenes only) are pre-verified Ahyeon windows already on disk.
- Flag kinds: `context` = CONTEXT — no public footage of the subject exists for this period · `era-anon` = ANONYMOUS-ERA — faces are unidentified trainees by design · `era-absence` = HIATUS-ERA — she objectively does not appear in this release (narration says so) · `wide-framing` = WIDE FRAMING — she is in the source; chosen windows are group wides.
