## 1. Debut & trainee years
- [2016-08-08] BLACKPINK debuted with single album "Square One", containing "Boombayah" and "Whistle"; first girl group to debut under YG Entertainment in seven years — Source: Wikipedia/Blackpink
- [2016-08] "Whistle" hit #1 on the Gaon (now Circle) Digital Chart; "Boombayah" hit #1 on Billboard World Digital Songs; BLACKPINK became the fastest act to hold the top two spots on World Digital Songs (after Psy and BIGBANG for YG) — Source: Wikipedia/Blackpink
- [2017-01-17] Fandom name "BLINK" (black + pink) revealed — Source: Wikipedia/Blackpink
- [2010] Jennie joined YG as a trainee in 2010 after returning from New Zealand (~6 years to debut); first member revealed June 1, 2016 — Source: Wikipedia/Blackpink + Wikipedia/Jennie
- [2011-07] Jisoo joined YG as a trainee in July 2011 (~5 years to debut); third member revealed June 15, 2016 — Source: Wikipedia/Blackpink
- [2012] Rosé ranked first among 700 applicants at YG's 2012 Australia audition and moved to Seoul (~4 years to debut); final member revealed June 22, 2016 — Source: Wikipedia/Blackpink
- [2010] Lisa was the only one of 4,000 applicants to pass YG's 2010 Thailand audition; moved to Korea in 2011 as YG's first non-ethnic-Korean trainee (~5 years to debut); second member revealed June 8, 2016 — Source: Wikipedia/Lisa + Wikipedia/Blackpink
- [2011-11-14] YG announced its new girl group as early as Nov 2011 (originally slated for 2012, at one point planned with 7–9 members); name "Blackpink" confirmed June 29, 2016 with 4 members; rejected names included Pink Punk, Baby Monster, Magnum — Source: Wikipedia/Blackpink
- [2016-11-25] Backdrop: YG's previous girl group 2NE1 disbanded (announced Nov 25, 2016) — months after BLACKPINK's debut; Minzy had left in April 2016, CL & Dara stayed, Bom was released; final single "Goodbye" (Jan 2017) — Source: Wikipedia/2NE1
- [2021] CL and Minzy later revealed they were not told of the disbandment beforehand; the news triggered public backlash against YG's mismanagement — Source: Wikipedia/2NE1
